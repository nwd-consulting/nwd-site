Gordon Tai

Global Partner | AI Enablement Specialist | STEM & Language Edupreneur

Gordon’s work spans three key pillars: international trade, technology, and education. In trade, he focuses on agri-commodities and energy, covering CIF sugar (ICUMSA) and CIF or TTT EN590 fuel; via CapFiX he connects trusted suppliers and buyers and facilitates structured, verifiable trade flows built on transparency and long-term value. In technology, Gordon leads AI enablement and transformation projects, integrating ERP systems and AI-driven workflows that streamline operations, enhance hiring, and drive scalability. In education, Gordon helps local and international students—particularly from China and EMEA regions—access English programs and university pathways in Malaysia, bridging global education with real-world opportunity through 3E English.
