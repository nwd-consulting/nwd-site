Services

Build the foundation for expansion & collaboration

NWD provides services across business, research, education, and technology. Let us build the foundation you need for expansion and collaboration.

International & Cross-Cultural Collaboration

Global work comes with real complexity. Our consultants have navigated international partnerships firsthand and can help you anticipate cultural dynamics — from relationship-building norms to the validity implications of working across contexts. NWD currently has connections in Australia, Brazil, Italy, Kenya, Malaysia, Nigeria, Pakistan, Peru, Portugal, Spain, South Africa, and the United States.

Research Methodology & Data Analysis

From early-stage design to final reporting, our psychometricians and quantitative analysts bring rigor to every phase. We help with research design, methodology, survey development, analytic models, data collection, A/B testing, and regression analysis — or uncovering what your existing data can tell you. Our consultants are well versed in human-subjects research, assessment development, hierarchical modeling, Item Response Theory, non-linear modeling, and more.

Writing, Review & Dissemination

Our technical writers and education experts help you communicate your work clearly and credibly. We support manuscript and dissertation writing, translate complex findings for broader audiences, and review surveys, pitches, presentations, and learning materials for audience fit, cultural sensitivity, grade-level alignment, DOK, and standards coverage.

Tech, Automation & AI Integration

Is more AI making a difference? Can you automate repetitive tasks at your company — with or without AI? Our AI psychometricians help you define what success actually looks like when using AI: reducing overhead, integrating data effectively, and measuring improvements at scale.

Education, Training & Curriculum Design

Whether you want to design employee training materials, K-12 standards-aligned coursework, a new EdTech tool, or AI-based lessons to enhance your curriculum, our experts build learning environments that make a difference. Our international team helps ensure your learning materials are well received.
