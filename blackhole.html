Past the event horizon

We take on the problems where the ordinary rules stop applying.

What's physically real here ▾
  • Light bending: each pixel's ray is integrated along a true null geodesic of the Schwarzschild metric (d²u/dφ² = −u + 1.5 rs u²), RK4-stepped — so you see a real Einstein ring and the disk's underside lensed over the top.
  • Photon ring & shadow: the black disk is the r = rs horizon; the bright ring at ~1.5 rs is trapped light.
  • Accretion disk: Keplerian flow with relativistic Doppler beaming (approaching side brighter/bluer, ∝ δ³), gravitational redshift √(1−rs/r), and a T ∝ r^−¾ profile.
  • The cube: gravitationally redshifts and dims, is tidally spaghettified (stretched radially), and — from our vantage — asymptotically freezes and reddens at the horizon rather than crossing.
